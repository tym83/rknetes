# Registry application templates

[Русская версия](README.ru.md)

This is an unsubmitted preparation package for RKNetes. It does not assert inclusion in the Russian software register, certification, eligibility of an unidentified rights holder, or exclusive ownership of upstream Kubernetes.

RKNetes is a modification and distribution of Apache-2.0 Kubernetes, built on kuberoot, with additional Go controllers, admission services, command-line tools and isolated network experiments. The source of RKNetes is licensed under Apache-2.0. Linux and bundled dependencies keep their own licences. The words “developed from scratch” and the “domestic conformance” story belong to the satire and are excluded from the application.

The applicant fills [application.md](application.md), supplies the evidence in [rights-and-components.md](rights-and-components.md), and completes [operations.md](operations.md) for its actual delivery and support arrangements. Empty fields are deliberately explicit. Only measured features and attached test results belong in the submitted product description.

The governing starting point is Government Decree No. 1236 of 16 November 2015. Use its current rules rather than its original procurement-ban title. The [official register's document catalogue](https://reestr.digital.gov.ru/documents/) lists No. 1236, classifier Order No. 486 of 22 September 2020 and amending Decree No. 1937 of 28 November 2025. The [official publication of No. 1937](https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202512080042) is a separate source from the [original No. 1236 publication page](https://government.ru/docs/20650/).
