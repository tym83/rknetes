Functional description: RKNetes deploys and manages container workloads through the Kubernetes API. Its optional lab agencies demonstrate network policy failure modes, IP reuse, admission restrictions, artifact redirects and inconsistencies between availability statements and measured service behavior. The destructive lab profile is isolated from the conformance profile. Evidence must identify which mechanisms passed the stand tests; creating a CRD does not demonstrate that its mechanism works.

The product incorporates upstream Kubernetes rather than replacing its authorship. Additional exclusive rights, if any, concern identifiable original modifications; upstream components remain available under their licences. Classification, rights-holder eligibility, revenue calculations and compatibility declarations require the applicant's actual evidence.
