# Specification completion backlog

[Русская версия](BACKLOG.ru.md)

The concept remains authoritative. A passing core experiment does not close every sentence of its contour. These entries identify the exact missing proof or incompatible claim, the reason it remains open, and the condition that closes it. They are not advertised as delivered features.
